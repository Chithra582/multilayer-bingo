# Game Commentary Engine Skill

## Overview
Enhances player immersion with dynamic announcements, calculating when players are '1 to go' and triggering victory fanfare.

## Operations
1. Analyzes active player cards against remaining required numbers.
2. Generates excitement alerts when players reach proximity thresholds.
3. Delivers congratulatory commentary upon verified Bingo.
